About

Building emotion intelligence
for content creators.

EmotionScope is a Block D Sprint 4 project built by NLP Group 1 at Breda University of Applied Sciences (BUas), in collaboration with the Content Intelligence Agency.

The project

Our client, the Content Intelligence Agency, analyses TV shows, podcasts and reality content for production studios across the Netherlands. They needed a way to quickly profile any piece of content: who's in it, where the energy peaks, what audience it fits, and how it stacks up against another show.

EmotionScope started as a 7-emotion classifier and grew into a full content-intelligence pipeline with 8 production features, 13 REST endpoints, and AI-generated narrative outputs.

Under the hood

●

Whisper

Speech-to-text on any audio or video file. Handles long-form English transcripts at ~60s per minute of audio.

●

MarianMT

Translates EN -> NL when needed for the Dutch market client.

●

DeBERTa

Fine-tuned on 7 emotions (happiness, neutral, anger, sadness, surprise, fear, disgust). The core classifier.

●

spaCy NER

Extracts named entities, characters, places, organizations, so we know who is in the content.

●

Groq · Llama 3.1

Generates AI titles, scene names, taglines, persuasion verdicts, and content classifications.

●

FastAPI + Vanilla JS

Python backend with FastAPI; no-framework frontend with Chart.js for visualizations.
